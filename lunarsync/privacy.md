# Lunar Sync – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

## What stays on your device

Your mood journal, chosen city, language and settings are stored only on your phone. We do not have servers and never receive this information. Deleting the app deletes it.

## Advertising

Lunar Sync is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location (from your IP address), app interactions and diagnostic data, and share them with Google and its advertising partners. Learn how Google uses this information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

If you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice any time in Settings, Privacy options. You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

## Notifications

Moon reminders are scheduled on your phone only if you turn them on. No data is sent anywhere.

## Location

The app does not access your location. Moonrise times use a city you pick yourself.

## Children

Lunar Sync is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
